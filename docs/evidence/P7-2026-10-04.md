# P7 — marks: drafted, submitted, corrected with a reason

Branch `p7-marks`, on main `6d19936` (P6). Target: schoolfees-dev
(`wtgxcptmucjerhufzjcf`) only. Synthetic exams for the QA teacher's class,
deleted after every run; ids, counts and outcomes only.

## What changed

**Four states per cell.**

- **Blank** means nobody has entered it.
- **Zero** is a mark.
- **Absent** means the child did not sit the paper.
- **Exempt** (new) means the child was excused from it.

An exempt paper is left out of both halves of a total, the way leave is in the
register. It is neither passed nor failed, and the report card, the CSV export
and the change log each name it. Before, there was nowhere to put an excused
child except a blank (so the paper never finished) or an absence (a nought on a
report).

**The paper carries its scheme.** Each paper has its maximum, its step (whole
marks, halves or quarters) and whether exemption is allowed. Once marks are
being entered, the step, the maximum and the exemption rule cannot be changed
under them.

**One command per paper: `save_marks`.**

- **Draft** keeps what is typed, blanks included, and returns a receipt.
- **Submit** is the teacher saying she has finished. It is refused while any
  child on the exam's roll is blank, naming them, and it approves nothing.
- **Correction** changes a submitted paper with a reason. It can never return a
  cell to blank, and the paper stays submitted.
- **Reopen** — the exam office hands a submitted paper back.

Every cell is checked against the scheme and the roll. Any refusal leaves the
whole column untouched: a valid cell sent beside an invalid one is not written
either. A repeated submission id returns the first receipt, and a save against
a revision somebody else has moved is refused as stale. A lost answer is asked
about with the same id, never resent.

**Who enters a paper.** The subject teacher the published timetable assigns to
that subject in that class, or the exam office (`assessments:manage`). A class
teacher reads her class's other subjects but cannot type into them. Before, any
teacher with any link to a class could enter every subject in it.

**The roll is the exam's**: enrolled by the time it ended, not gone before it
began. Without exam dates, it is today's roll.

**The missing-marks nudge** counts a paper outstanding until it is submitted,
and asks the subject teacher (the class teacher only where nobody teaches the
subject). Its paper ids are chunked (rule 3b).

`verify:marks` (23):

- A draft keeps a zero, an absence and an exemption distinct and writes nothing
  for a blank.
- A replay writes nothing, a reused id is refused, and a stale revision is
  refused.
- A mark above the maximum refuses the whole column; a quarter on a half-step
  paper is refused; a child not on the roll is refused.
- Submitting with blanks is refused, names them, and writes nothing. A
  no-change draft is refused.
- A complete submit records who submitted.
- After submitting: a draft is refused; a correction needs a reason, cannot
  blank a cell, and is logged with its reason while the paper stays submitted.
- The class teacher cannot enter a subject she does not teach, nor can another
  teacher. The exam office can enter any paper, and only the exam office
  reopens.
- A no-exemption paper refuses exempt, and a whole-mark paper refuses a half.
- Faults after marks and before the receipt leave the column untouched.
- Two simultaneous saves give one that lands and one stale.
- A child admitted after the exam is not on its roll.
- A locked exam refuses every save.

The browser journeys (3):

- The subject teacher drafts, is shown the blanks and cannot submit, completes
  the column, submits, then corrects with a reason. The log keeps the reason.
- A lost draft answer is confirmed, not resent: one command.
- The class teacher sees another subject read-only.

## Not established

  CBSE-style bands.
- Deadline grace and escalation. The nudge still drafts messages weekly; it is
  not yet an outbox notice.
- Phone trials with subject teachers (R8.3).
