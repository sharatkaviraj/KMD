# P3, P4, P5 — absence to cover to notice to teacher, 2026-10-04

The first complete workflow of the planning pack (§5, §8, §9; coding prompts
5, 6 and 7): an operator saves an absence; safe future cover is assigned and
published; the assigned staff are queued a notice; the teacher opens one
authenticated duty from Today or the link, and acknowledges it or says she
cannot cover. Branch `p3-cover-command`. Target schoolfees-dev only. No real
WhatsApp transport exists on any deployment (P0 manifest); dev uses the mock.

## P3 — Gate E: one atomic, versioned, idempotent command

**Before.** Saving an absence, generating (select, delete, insert, stamp),
editing and publishing were separate requests with no transaction. A failure
between delete and insert left teachers seeing nothing; two coordinators
interleaved; publishing never re-checked the absence; a manual cover was never
validated against the timetable; nobody was told.

**Now.** Every cover change — save absence, back at school, assign, release,
arrange again — is one call to `vppsone.commit_cover_command`. The server reads
the day, applies the operator's intent in memory, arranges the whole day
(`domain/arrange.ts`) and hands the *desired state of every future duty* to the
function, which in one transaction:

1. refuses an actor without `substitutions:manage` (re-read now, not from the page);
2. serialises the day on an advisory lock;
3. returns the stored receipt for a repeated submission id (same payload) and
   refuses a reused id with a different one;
4. refuses a stale plan: the day's revision, the published timetable or the
   bells changed since the operator looked;
5. writes the absence and appends its history (no reason is asked or stored);
6. re-validates every assignment against the facts now — active, present, not
   teaching, not double booked, period not begun — and raises, rolling the
   absence back with everything else, if one fails;
7. diffs desired against live in two phases (end the changed rows, then write
   the new ones, so a teacher moved between rooms in one period never collides
   with herself); unchanged duties keep their identity and are not re-announced;
   begun periods are history and are never touched;
8. writes the notice intents the changes need, withdrawing an unsent notice
   rather than sending a correction for it;
9. bumps the day's revision and stores the receipt.

**The arranger** keeps still-valid covers with their teachers (stability),
releases a pinned cover whose teacher is now away or teaching, allocates the
rest with the existing engine (tiers, familiarity, fairness, min-cost flow),
then checks the whole plan again — releasing and re-allocating, latest period
first, any cover that breaks a rule only in combination (the audit's P1-P2-P5
consecutive-run counterexample is a test). Nothing becomes "self study" or
"merged" by itself any more: what cannot be arranged safely is `needs_help`
with the reason and, where the engine has one, a suggestion. Only teaching
staff are automatic candidates; a person may still choose anybody active.

**The screen** (`/substitutions`) has no draft and no publish button. Its
receipt separates assigned, needs help and notices queued ("4 assigned · 1
needs help · notices queued to 3 teachers · 1 without a phone — assigned,
notice blocked"). A lost response shows "Checking whether this saved…" and asks
the server before it offers to try again with the same submission id.

## P4 — Gate F: a staff outbox that does not lie

`staff_notices` are intents written by the command. `staff_notice_attempts` is
the ledger of trying. The dispatcher (`staff-notices/data/dispatcher.ts`) takes
one notice at a time through three database steps: **claim** under a lease
after re-checking it (duty still theirs, period not ended, active, has a
number — otherwise suppressed with the reason), **stamp** what is about to be
sent before it is sent, **finish** with what came back. Only a provider receipt
id is acceptance; a timeout or a 2xx without an id is **Unknown** and is never
retried by a machine. Rate limits and provider errors retry with bounded
backoff while the notice still means something. A person settles an Unknown:
"received (checked in person)" or "send again — may arrive twice", recorded as
theirs. Notices go out after the command's response (`after()`), from a "Send
now" button, and from a 07:30 job; a provider outage cannot undo a plan.

Transport is a deployment fact: `STAFF_NOTICE_TRANSPORT` = `disabled` | `mock`
| `aisensy`. A dev deployment defaults to the mock; a live one to disabled;
`aisensy` needs an API key and never runs under a test runner. Template names
and six positional parameters are fixed, but **no AiSensy campaign has been
created, approved or verified** — that is the separate provider gate.

## P5 — Gate G: the teacher's duty

`/duty/[id]` is where the WhatsApp link and Today both lead. Sign-in first (the
proxy returns her to the link afterwards). The row decides, not the link: the
current holder sees the duty and two buttons; whoever held an older revision
sees that it changed or was cancelled; the office sees it; anybody else —
including someone a link was forwarded to — sees "This link is not for your
account" and nothing more. Opening the page changes nothing.

**Acknowledge** records that she has seen it (not that she is in the room).
**I cannot cover** records it and escalates to the academic coordinators (or the
administrators when there are none); the duty stays hers until a command
reassigns it, and the arranger never asks her for it again.

## Incident during this work

Applying P3's migration added `teacher_absences.updated_by → staff_profiles`, a
second foreign key to the same table. The deployed (P1) build's unnamed
`staff_profiles!inner(...)` embed on that table became ambiguous and
`/substitutions` on vppsone.vercel.app errored for about twenty minutes until
the constraint was dropped (`*_absence_updated_by_is_not_an_embed`). The app's
own embed now names its constraint; `tests/embeds.test.ts` would have caught it
had it been run on the branch first. Dev only, Sunday, no data affected.
