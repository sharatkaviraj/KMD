# P8 — SA1 report cards: scheme, completeness, immutable issued revision

Branch `p8-reports`, on main `12de230` (P7). Target: schoolfees-dev
(`wtgxcptmucjerhufzjcf`) only. Synthetic terms (`QA-P8-…`, `QA-P8E-…`) and
synthetic fixtures; nothing printed but ids, counts and outcomes.

## What changed

**A card is worked out from a pinned scheme.** `report_schemes` holds, per
term:

- the grade bands;
- how many decimals the percentage is rounded to (half up, on the decimal
  value) before it is graded;
- an optional pass percentage;
- the attendance reporting period;
- the English and Hindi header;
- the signature lines.

One revision can be a draft at a time. An approved revision never changes,
because a trigger refuses it, and a change after approval becomes the next
revision. The scheme screen at `/assessments/scheme` is read by whoever reads
reports and written by the exam office. Nobody at the school has confirmed its
bands yet, so the default is the CBSE-style set this app has always shown, and
the screen says so.

**A card says what it is.**

- Absent and exempt are named on the card, never turned into a number.
- An exempt paper is out of both halves of the total.
- The percentage is the unrounded fraction rounded once, at the scheme's place,
  and the grade is taken from that rounded number.
- Attendance is counted over the reporting period on the days the register was
  recorded. The school days it was not recorded are shown beside it and never
  counted as present.
- The card prints the scheme's header and signature lines.
- A card that has not been issued shows **DRAFT — not issued**, on screen and
  on paper.

**Issuing is one command, `issue_report_card`.** It re-checks everything below
and refuses the card if any check fails:

- the actor runs the exams;
- the scheme is the term's latest approved revision;
- every paper the class sat that term has been submitted by its teacher;
- no paper has moved since the card was worked out (paper entry revisions are
  carried as the card's sources);
- nothing is missing;
- the revision the page showed is still current.

Each refusal is named, with the subjects concerned where that helps. A
**reissue** must give a reason. It supersedes the old revision, links to it and
keeps it; the history list shows every revision with who, when and why. Issued
rows are append-only: a trigger refuses any edit except issued → superseded,
and refuses deletes, except for synthetic `QA-` terms that the tests create and
remove. A repeated submission id returns the first result.

The one card already issued on dev was imported as revision 1, kind
`imported`. The old `report_cards` table is kept, with a comment, but is no
longer written.

`verify:reports` (14):

- A card cannot be issued against a draft scheme, or while a paper is
  unsubmitted (naming it).
- An incomplete card is refused, and so is one from marks that moved (naming
  the paper).
- A teacher cannot issue.
- Revision 1 is issued; a replay returns it; a reused id is refused.
- A reissue without a reason is refused, and so is one against a stale revision.
- A fault after the supersede leaves revision 1 issued.
- A reissue becomes revision 2, with revision 1 superseded and linked.
- An issued card cannot be edited or brought back, and an approved scheme
  cannot be edited.
- A newer approved scheme stops issuing against the old one.

## Not established

- **The school's own SA1 scheme.** Its subjects and components, maxima,
  weights, grade bands, rounding place, pass rule, header wording and
  signatories are not confirmed; the defaults stand until the exam office
  approves its own. This is the plan's "configuration to confirm" table, and
  it needs the school.
- Long-name and Hindi page wrapping were not checked in print against a real
  printer; the card prints through the browser.
- A PDF file reference is not stored. The issued revision's JSON is the record,
  and printing re-renders it.
- Bulk issue for a whole class is not built; cards are issued one child at a
  time.
