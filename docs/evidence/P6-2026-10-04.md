# P6 — attendance never invents presence

Branch `p6-attendance`, on main `0824721` (P3–P5). Target: schoolfees-dev
(`wtgxcptmucjerhufzjcf`) only. Synthetic QA accounts and the de-identified
replica's children; nothing printed but ids, counts and outcomes.

## What changed

**Nothing is recorded until somebody saves.** Opening a register writes
nothing. Every child shows as present only as a preview. The teacher marks the
exceptions: absent, **half day** (new), late or leave. A review screen lists the
class, the date, the totals and every child who is not present. Only **Save
attendance** records it. An unsaved register is outstanding, never "all
present".

**A save is the whole roll for that date.** `save_attendance_register` is one
transaction. It re-checks who is acting, that the date has happened and the
school was open, and that the marks name every child on the roll *for that
date*, exactly once — the roll comes from the enrolment record. A child
admitted after the date is not on it, and one who left that day still is. It
also re-checks the revision the page showed. Then it writes the register, every
mark and a revision row in one go and returns a receipt: who, when, the totals
and the revision.

- A changed roll is refused, naming who joined or left.
- A register somebody else saved meanwhile is refused as stale, showing their
  receipt.
- A repeated submission id returns the first receipt. A reused id with a
  different payload is refused.

**Lost answers are asked about, not resent.** The browser mints one submission
id when the review opens. If the answer never arrives, the screen says
"Checking whether your save completed", asks the server about that id, and
offers Try again only if it did not land — with the same id, so it cannot save
twice. Marks wait on the phone while offline but are never sent without a
person pressing Save; the old offline auto-replay queue (never imported) is
deleted.

**Corrections are revisions with a reason.**

- The class teacher (or assistant) *as of today* and the office correct
  directly. The review lists exactly what changes, and a reason is required.
  Revision n+1 records the before-and-after; revision n is kept and shown in a
  History list.
- A subject teacher reads the classes she teaches and may *ask* for a
  correction; approving that request is the same command, so the correction and
  the request's approval commit together.
- A former class teacher keeps nothing.

**Half day is the school's rule.** `attendance_rules.half_day_credit`, per
session, defaults to 0.5 and is set on /academics by `academics:manage`. The
register, the student card, the monthly sheet and the report card all read the
one rule. The monthly sheet also keeps children who left during the month,
rather than dropping their marked days.

**The reminder goes through the staff outbox.** The job now runs at 04:00 UTC
(09:30 in school, after the first period by the default bells) and asks nobody
before the first period has ended by the bells. It queues an
`attendance_reminder` notice to whoever answers for each outstanding register
*on that day* (class teacher, else assistant), keyed by class, day and person,
so re-running it adds nothing. The outbox re-checks at the moment it sends: a
register saved since, or a teacher no longer responsible, is suppressed. The
link opens the register, which says "Recorded" if it was saved in the meantime.

**The deploy window heals itself.** A register the previous build saved in the
old way (`submitted`, revision 0, no history) is imported as revision 1 the
moment anybody saves. That save is told it is stale rather than writing over
it. None existed when checked.

`verify:attendance` (22): nothing recorded before a save; the class teacher's
save covers every child at revision 1 with a receipt; a replay writes nothing; a
reused id is refused; a stale save shows the receipt it lost to; a missing child
is refused and named; a stranger is refused; a child admitted after the date is
off that date's roll; a correction with no reason is refused; a correction with
no change is refused; a correction is revision 2 with reason and before-after,
revision 1 kept; a subject teacher can neither save nor correct; the office's
save records on-behalf-of the class teacher; future and closed days are refused;
injected faults after register, after marks and before receipt leave nothing;
request approval and correction commit together and cannot apply twice; two
simultaneous first saves give one recorded and one stale; a register from the
old build is imported and the next save is stale; a reminder is suppressed when
already saved or sent to someone not responsible; and the half-day credit is
read from the rule.

The e2e journeys (3):

- The class teacher saves with one absent and one half day, gets a receipt,
  then corrects with a reason. Both revisions are kept and History shows them.
- A save whose answer is dropped (the request reached the server, the response
  never reached the page) is confirmed by asking, not resent: one revision.
- teacher2 cannot take or correct the class's register.

## Behaviour that changed for people

- **A subject teacher no longer takes a class's daily register.** It belongs to
  the class teacher or the assistant, or to the office on their behalf. This is
  the plan's rule ("daily attendance belongs to the class teacher or explicit
  delegate"); before, anyone with a teaching assignment in the class could.
- **There is no "All present" button**; everyone is present until changed.
  There is no "Submit"; there is "Review and save".
- The reminder moves from 11:00 to after the first period.

## Not established

- Physical phones and nontechnical-teacher trials (R8.3). The plan's target —
  ≥90% finishing a 40-pupil register unaided, median under 90 s — is untested.
- The today-register journey is skipped on Sundays and was not run today; its
  steps are covered on past days by `attendance-register.spec.ts`.
- Whether the school wants a half day at 0.5 is its decision; 0.5 is the
  default until somebody changes it on /academics.
- A class whose children move to another class mid-session: the enrolment
  record keeps one class per child per session, so a past register for the old
  class is kept as saved, but that child's dated roll for those days follows
  the current class. Recorded as a known limit.
