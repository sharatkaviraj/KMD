## P1 — Gate C: one authority, and reads scoped to it

### What was open

On dev, before this change, every one of the 39 `vppsone` tables carried
`for select to authenticated using (true)` with a table-wide SELECT grant —
read from `pg_policies` and `information_schema.role_table_grants` on dev
itself, not inferred from the migrations. The direct-API verifier below was
written after that and first run after the row policies were applied, so the
"before" is what those policies and grants permit, not a measured count:

- a login with **no staff profile** — what every fee-app-only account is —
  could read every enrolment, register, note, mark and guardian snapshot, and
  every collection link's bearer token;
- so could the **deactivated** QA teacher for as long as the JWT lived, and the
  accountant, the fee collector and the viewer;
- the office could promote any linked colleague to administrator, or switch an
  administrator off, through `/staff` (source: `updateStaffAction` accepted any
  role; only self-changes were refused).

### What changed

- `vppsone.role_permissions` is `permissions.ts` as rows (93), and helper
  functions answer the app's own questions in SQL: active staff,
  `staff_can`, `reads_every_class`, `my_class_keys` (class teacher over the
  whole effective interval, plus the published timetable; never a cover duty).
- Every `using (true)` policy is replaced. Reference data needs an active
  profile; pupil data needs the permission its screen needs **and** the class
  scope; collection data needs `collection:view` or being the assignee.
- Phones, absence reasons and link tokens stop being readable directly
  (second migration, applied after the code that reads them moved). The app
  reads them through the service role after `staff:view`,
  `substitutions:manage` and `collection:view` respectively.
- `change_staff_authority` is the only way to change a role or switch access:
  admin-only, re-reads the actor at call time, refuses self-changes, appends
  to `staff_authority_changes`, and a trigger holds the last active
  administrator under an advisory lock. The office keeps editing records and
  may create only teacher and view-only profiles (D-205).
- The teacher pages now refuse other classes instead of rendering them:
  class roll, child card (guardian names and phones), monthly register,
  attendance, the corrections queue, correction requests (which also read the
  register's real status instead of assuming "submitted"), and notes. Register
  and marks saves refuse a child not on the roll. Issuing a report card needs
  `assessments:manage` (D-206).

### Not run, and why

- **Two simultaneous admin demotions against a live connection pair.** Proving
  it on dev means first switching every real administrator off outside a
  transaction, which would lock the school out of its own test site if the
  test failed. The guarantee rests on the trigger taking one advisory lock and
  counting after it, which is the standard serialisation; the single-session
  refusal is covered by the command's own path. Recorded as never run.
- Physical-device and nontechnical-staff checks belong to Gate G.
- `private.current_staff_role()` still prefers an active `public.users` row and
  falls through an inactive one (D-191). Changing a shared fee function is
  outside this authorization; the consequence and the decision it needs are in
  D-207.
