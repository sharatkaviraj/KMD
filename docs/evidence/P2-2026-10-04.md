# P2 — physical teaching events, bells and team rules, 2026-10-04

Gates B and D of the planning pack (§6, §7; coding prompts 2 and 4). Branch
`p2-physical-duties`, stacked on P0/P1. Target schoolfees-dev only.

## What the flat timetable lost, and what now carries it

`timetable_slots` is one row per teacher × class × period. Cover was grouped
by `classId|period`, so:

| Shape | Before | Now (`domain/events.ts`) |
|---|---|---|
| Joint lesson — one teacher, several sections | one vacancy **per section**; one cover teacher could not take them all (no-double-booking index), so two of three went to self study | one `joint` event; one cover need; the substitute is ranked against **every** section and takes the worst tier (`alsoClassNames` in the engine) |
| Split class — two cohorts, two teachers | both halves away collapsed to **one** vacancy named by the first teacher | two `parallel_cohort` events; each away teacher is its own need |
| Team block (ELGA) | a vacancy only when **every** teacher was away; one of five away was silently "covered" | a `team_block`; with no school rule any absence is a visible **needs check**, never arranged by the app; with a rule ("4 of 5 present") the shortfall is counted exactly |
| Same subject, same time, two teachers | the clash checker assumed one combined room | two events, always; grouping is only ever forced by one *person* in several classes |
| One person in two different lessons | invisible | reported as a clash on the rules page |

The engine's class familiarity also undercounted joint lessons: the teacher's
schedule kept only the last row written for a period. `teacherDetailsFrom` now
carries the other sections (`alsoClassNames`), so a teacher of 11 Science,
Commerce and Arts together is familiar with all three.

## School facts the timetable cannot hold

- **Bells** (`bell_schedules`, one current row per session, versioned).
  Seeded with the regular profile Raj chose: eight 40-minute periods from
  08:30, break 11:10–11:30, ending 14:10, Asia/Kolkata. The six-period heatwave
  profile is not reintroduced. Editable at `/substitutions/rules`.
- **Team rules** (`team_supervision_rules`): how many of a team must be
  present. None are set — that is a school decision, and until it is made ELGA
  stays manual exactly as Timetable Studio leaves it.
- **Eligibility.** Only teaching staff are automatic candidates (P3 uses this).
  An active office profile is not evidence of classroom eligibility.

## Tests

- `tests/teaching-events.test.ts` — synthetic Teachers A–H: ordinary, joint,
  parallel, team, same-subject-two-rooms, clash, key stability, other days;
  needs for joint/parallel/team with and without rules, partial-day absence,
  need-key stability; every-section ranking (a teacher who knows one of three
  sections ranks `exact`, the one who knows all three `class_subject`); one
  teacher allocated to a joint lesson, counted once.
- `tests/bells.test.ts` — school clock from a UTC server, the default profile,
  future/started/over, unknown periods are never "safe", break-adjacent period,
  invalid bells refused.
- Existing engine, plan, fixture and parser suites unchanged and green.
