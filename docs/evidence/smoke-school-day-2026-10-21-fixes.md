# Smoke school day — what was fixed (21 October 2026)

Every one of the 262 findings from the 20 October run
(`smoke-school-day-2026-10-20.md`), with what happened to it. Eight
developer agents fixed one area each in its own worktree, and the lead
merged them and finished the hand-offs between them (branch
`fix/smoke-school-day`).

"Improved" means the problem is answered but not every part the finding
asked for (each note says what is left). "By design" and "Not reproducible"
say why. "Needs owner" is a school decision; the code does the safe thing
until it is made.
