Improve the VPPS One application already built. Preserve its native MyFees workspace, school identity, timetable and SamPark compatibility. ## 2 Decisions already made and still needed

### Already settled

VPPS One is a personalized single-school tool. Keep the current application and fee port. Prioritize absence-to-cover automation. Use English-first labels with Hindi help. Daily attendance belongs to the class teacher or explicit delegate, supports half-day and flexible audited correction, and needs a reminder after the applicable first period. Subject teachers enter their own marks and notes. Parents do not need logins. Parent messages need approval by default, with configurable bounded exceptions.

### Recommended implementation order

| Step | Result | Why it comes first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Confirm source, isolation, access and policy | No new workflow should widen staff or financial exposure |
| 1 | Physical teaching duties and atomic cover commit | A class-period row cannot safely represent every joint or ELGA lesson |
| 2 | Staff notices and current duty loop | WhatsApp is useful only when cancellation, failure and responsibility are clear |
| 3 | Attendance, marks and report revisions | Reliable records make reminders and parent output meaningful |
| 4 | Controlled parent messages, parity and archives | Complete the surrounding operations without changing financial contracts |

### Only unresolved decisions

- Who may mark absence, resolve unmatched duties and authorize cover policy; eligibility by grade/subject, non-teaching blocks, room/group supervision, workload and movement rules

- Actual bell/calendar authority, half-day accounting, closed-year correction scope and acknowledgement/escalation policy

- Current approved staff campaigns, task-link support and delivery reconciliation; verified staff/guardian routes and category budgets

- Assessment components, rounding and publication authority; parent report format/access; archive ownership and hosting eligibility

The session agenda on page 23 converts these questions into a concrete first test. Do not reopen the settled product preferences merely because an older document says something different.

## 4 A short workspace for nontechnical staff

The teacher mental model is simple: the school sends today's work, I check the date and class, do it, save it, and know it is done. Personalization should preselect the teacher's own classes, subjects and timetable. It should not move controls around or expose a large ERP menu.

Diagram: Today → current Cover duty → explicit Attendance saved receipt. Identity, class/date, status and Hindi help remain visible.

Synthetic storyboard. These screens show information hierarchy, not a final UI skin. Keep the current Ledger Calm visual language and existing form components.

### Everyday navigation

Use Today, My class and More. For a teacher without class-teacher responsibility, show My teaching instead. Coordinator tools sit behind their role; accountants retain the familiar fee workspace. Staff identity and readable school date remain visible on shared devices.

### Interaction rules

- Open the exact task from WhatsApp; after sign-in return to it. Today provides the same task if the notice never arrives

- One clear primary action per step; large controls, visible words for Saved, Not saved, Needs help and Delivery not confirmed

- Use Hindi help beside the task, preserve existing language choices, and validate vocabulary with school staff

- No mandatory installation, swipe-only actions, tiny horizontal marks grids or assumption that WhatsApp webview and home-screen app share sessions

### Proposed usability gate

Test 5-8 representative staff, including least-confident Android users and an iPhone user. Measure independent completion, wrong-date errors and support interventions. Proposed attendance target: at least 90% correctly finish a synthetic 40-pupil task after brief training; median under 90 seconds excluding login. These are test targets, not promised results.

## 5 The first complete absence workflow

Diagram: Select absence → validate and commit current physical duties → Today plus staff WhatsApp. Assigned, unresolved and notice evidence remain separate.

### The operator action

Select absent teacher, school date, full day or periods. Show the current affected duties without asking for a medical reason. Save absence & arrange cover validates the latest school facts, assigns feasible future duties under the agreed policy, publishes them and queues staff notices. Routine valid duties need no extra per-period approval.

### The receipt tells the truth

Example: Absence saved; 4 duties assigned; 1 needs help; notices queued to 3 teachers. Assigned, unresolved and notification counts stay separate. Missing phone means Assigned; notification blocked. Missing or stale timetable means Needs timetable check, never an invented free teacher.

### The teacher loop

Today and the WhatsApp link open the same authenticated current duty: class/group, subject or supervision type, period/time, room, and version. Acknowledge records awareness. I cannot cover creates visible reassignment need. It does not silently transfer responsibility or free the interval before a new plan commits.

### Changes are ordinary cases

Teacher return, substitute absence, timetable/room change and wrong-teacher correction revise relevant future duties. Preserve completed-period history. Cancel obsolete queued notices and prepare explicit changes for already-notified staff. Old links show Cancelled or the current duty. A past correction must not send a go-to-class-now instruction.

WhatsApp delivery or a blue tick is not proof of acknowledgement or classroom coverage. An imminent unresolved or unacknowledged duty needs a named operator and agreed in-person/contact fallback, rather than a success badge.

## 6 Model the actual physical teaching duty

The parser recognizes joint lessons, but import/storage flattens teacher x class x period and vacancies use classId and period. Joint duties can duplicate, parallel cohorts collapse and remaining ELGA teachers seem to prove supervision. These are source-derived gaps, not live incidents.

Diagram: Multiple class timetable views → one true physical event/cohort/team → one cover resource claim. Parallel rooms are distinct; ELGA supervision explicit.

### Add a normalized event projection

Keep current timetable versions, slots, parser and views. Bind them to a stable physical event: interval, room/location, participating cohorts/classes, required teacher team, minimum supervision, subject/supervision type and provenance. One event may appear in several class views but occupies a substitute once.

| Synthetic example | Required representation | Wrong shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joint senior lesson | One shared event, both cohorts, actual room/capacity | Two independent substitutes for the same physical lesson |
| Parallel ELGA groups | Separate physical groups and required supervision | Any remaining teacher means all children are covered |
| Same subject at same time | Distinct rooms/events unless actually shared | Subject similarity cancels a physical clash |
| Two absent team members | All affected groups and minimum team coverage | One class-period vacancy hides uncovered groups |

### School confirmation is essential

The school must identify real joint/group arrangements, minimum supervision, blocked non-teaching duties and any permitted supervised merging. Same-time same-subject rows alone cannot establish physical grouping. Unsupervised self-study stays unresolved unless an explicit school policy defines a feasible supervised alternative.

Studio is now eight-period only; old heatwave plans are superseded. Its ELGA team cover stays manual because cohort duties are unmapped. Its Arts option model is disabled and persistence strips it. Do not import that normalizer into the new cohort store; unknown group supervision stays with the coordinator.

## 7 Allocate feasible cover before optimizing fairness

### Hard constraints at commitment

- Active eligible teacher; no absence or blocked teaching, cover, supervision, meeting or non-teaching duty

- Full interval occupancy, school-defined movement/break buffer and valid school calendar/bell version

- Grade/subject/supervision eligibility, shift, maximum and consecutive duty rules where approved

- All physical cohorts and team-supervision requirements, room/group capacity and feasible locations

- Manual pins remain only when currently valid; unknown availability or eligibility remains unresolved

### Then apply preferences

Prefer subject continuity, familiarity with class, balanced recent cover load, fewer room changes and stable earlier assignments. Batch simultaneous absences and protect scarce candidates before assigning easy duties. Deterministic tie-breaking and recorded policy versions let the office understand why a teacher was selected.

### A final whole-plan validation is mandatory

A locally valid next assignment can make the final consecutive-duty run invalid. A stale allocation can clash with a new absence or manual duty. Recheck all affected resource intervals and the entire final plan at commit, including unchanged pins. Reserve ownership so two operators cannot publish competing claims on the same teacher.

| Outcome | Office sees | Allowed next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feasible cover | Assigned automatically and simple eligibility reason | Notice and teacher acknowledgement flow |
| No eligible candidate | Needs help with factual blockers | Coordinator chooses a feasible authorized alternative |
| Unknown input | Needs timetable or availability check | Resolve data or confirm policy before automation |
| Policy exception | Reason, actor and explicit feasible supervision type | Named decision; never override physical impossibility |

A generic Override cannot place one teacher in two rooms at once. Safe unresolved duties are a valid output; fictional coverage is not. Do not infer teacher competence from casual notes or student sensitive attributes.

## 8 One transactional command and one durable receipt

Diagram: Current staff app and scoped command → atomic owned-domain absence/duty/publication/outbox/receipt → asynchronous guarded dispatcher. Existing fee contracts remain isolated.

### Command contract

Input: actor, logical submission ID, expected absence/timetable/policy/plan versions, date and absence intervals. Server derives affected future events and candidates. It must never trust browser-posted eligibility, class scope or final assignment facts without recomputation.

In one owned-schema transaction: validate actor and versions; preserve valid pins; reserve teacher intervals; save absence revision; save validated changed duties and publication revision; add notice intents; persist the command receipt. An injected failure leaves the previous publication intact. A version conflict returns review-needed rather than last-write-wins.

### Retry and concurrency

The same logical submission returns its committed receipt. If the response is lost, the UI checks that receipt before creating another request. Different concurrent commands serialize relevant resources and compare current inputs. A later intentional correction receives a new logical command and revision.

### Independent boundaries

Saving a valid plan and sending a message are different operations. The dispatcher runs after commit; a provider outage cannot undo school coverage. A global plan version change does not renotify unchanged duties. Stable duty identity and changed assignment facts determine notices.

The architecture extends existing VPPS domain/action/data boundaries and job infrastructure. Use additive operational tables in the owned vppsone schema. Do not introduce another authentication system, duplicate student master, second fee engine or separate teacher database.

## 9 WhatsApp needs a reliable operational outbox

Reuse reviewed AiSensy transport and registry concepts, not the fee-day log or retry behavior unchanged. Fee code has initial claim-before-send, family routing, parameter snapshots and CSV reconciliation. It also maps ambiguous network outcomes to failed and has unreserved stale retry paths. The new staff layer must correct those boundaries.

### Logical notice and attempts

Notice identity combines the stable duty or record revision, notice category and intended staff identity. Snapshot actual destination/contact version, template, language, parameters, policy and approval. Every dispatch attempt has its own lease and result; it is not a new duty or notice.

| Before dispatch | After provider call | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recheck current duty, owner, phone, policy, template and timing | Accepted with provider reference if supplied | Reconcile delivered/read separately |
| Atomically claim one attempt | Definite rejection or rate limit | Bounded retry only with current facts and guard rules |
| Cancel obsolete unsent work | Timeout or crash after request may mean accepted | Unknown; reconcile, or show uncertainty before authorized resend |
| Protect actual sent history | Delayed or replayed callbacks | Monotonic evidence; never reactivate an old duty |

### Capabilities still requiring account verification

Approved staff-cover wording, variable order, language, task URL/button, media, webhook authentication, per-recipient status lookup and actual billing are not established by an adapter or analytics page. SamPark has a URL-button implementation; that does not prove the VPPS domain/template is approved.

Use staff operational notices automatically only under the configured policy. Keep parent approval separately enabled by default. No exactly-once WhatsApp promise: an external provider timeout can remain unknowable. Teachers can always open current work in Today.

Sensitive reasons and student names do not belong in cover notices. Separate school record, teacher action and communication evidence throughout the UI.

## 10 Access correctness is a pilot prerequisite

The source audit found three important gates: authenticated SELECT USING true in sampled academic/token tables; office staff-management accepting another profile's role change; and native versus fee staff writers with different role authority. Actual deployed policies, grants and contradictory records were not inspected. These are source-confirmed control paths, with consequences to verify later.

### Unify authority deliberately

Use active native staff profiles as the app authority, subject to an explicit compatibility decision for fee RPCs. D-191 currently prefers active public.users, then native profiles; app admission reads native profiles. Test conflicting roles, inactive legacy records and deactivation. Ordinary profile edits must not grant role, login-link, credential or administrator powers. Preserve at least one active administrator.

| Responsibility | Permitted scope | Not granted by inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class teacher | Effective class/date register, bounded history and comments | Other classes, finance edits or guardian export |
| Subject teacher | Assigned subject/class/assessment marks and notes | Daily register simply from teaching membership |
| Cover teacher | Specific current physical teaching duty | Class-teacher history/attendance authority |
| Academic or exam coordinator | Explicit academic/exam management scope | Finance or credentials by default |
| Accountant or collector | Existing fee capability and real financial actor | Universal academic/private-note access |

Every read, save, approval, export and download checks current actor, action and row scope. Narrow direct PostgREST/RLS reads and hide bearer tokens from generic reads. ## 12 Attendance must never invent presence

### A class teacher marks exceptions then saves

Opening Today or a roster creates no attendance record. Present on save is a draft preview. Mark absent or half-day, review class/date/full roster and exceptions, then explicitly Save attendance. Only the server receipt makes the register recorded. The receipt shows actor, time, totals and revision.

| State | What the system may say | What it must not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unopened or unsaved | Not recorded; attendance task outstanding | Everyone present; no parent absence event |
| Saved whole roster | Actual present/absent/half-day under school rules | Hidden filtered pupils omitted |
| Save outcome unknown | Checking whether your save completed | Failed for sure; post a new request blindly |
| Corrected revision | Before/after, reason and actor | Old record or issued report quietly overwritten |

### Effective date and correction

Roster membership uses actual enrolment on the register date. A transfer/admission change or newer register revision forces review of differences. Teacher own-scope correction is flexible and audited; closed-year access needs a separate school policy. A former class teacher does not keep indefinite access merely because they once taught the class.

### Half-day and reminders

Keep arrival-late and departure-early details as configured. The school decides whether half-day counts as half-credit, lateness metadata or another accounting rule. Reports must use that rule consistently. Remind only current responsible staff after the applicable first period ends, accounting for holiday/exam/special bell schedules. Current jobs use an 11 AM India-time reminder and draft rather than dispatch academic notices; this needs adaptation.

A save just before dispatch suppresses pending reminders. A previously accepted reminder may still arrive; its link opens Already saved. A date/deadline change preserves task identity and drafts. No task is completed merely because a due time passed.

## 13 Marks and SA1 reports need explicit school rules

### Teacher marks entry

Open only the assigned class, subject, assessment and component. Use one component at a time on a phone. Preserve blank, zero, absent and exempt/not applicable as distinct values. Save draft returns a draft receipt. Submit marks deliberately completes the teacher task under the allowed completeness rule. Submission does not approve a result or publish a report. ## 1. Vercel: eligibility, scheduler and minimum paid cost

### Eligibility gate

Hobby is $0, restricted to **non-commercial personal use**. Vercel's definition of commercial use includes financial gain by anyone involved, including a paid employee/consultant building it, and sites requesting or processing payments. Commercial usage requires Pro or Enterprise. Its guidance directs uncertain cases to Vercel Support. This school app's fee/operational purpose warrants checking the actual owner and use against those terms; no educational exception was established by this research. [Hobby plan](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby), [fair-use guidance, updated 14 September 2026](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines)

### Cron restrictions

| Plan | Jobs per project | Minimum interval | Execution precision |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hobby | 100 | Once per day per job | Within the scheduled hour |
| Pro / Enterprise | 100 | Once per minute | Within the scheduled minute |

More-frequent Hobby expressions fail deployment. A Hobby 08:00 job may run between 08:00:00 and 08:59:59; Pro per-minute precision is not an exact-second guarantee. Cron has no separate job fee, but invokes Functions and consumes their usage. [Cron usage/pricing, updated 15 July 2026](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing), [execution behavior, updated 11 August 2026](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs)

Vercel describes delivery as best effort: a scheduled request can be missed or duplicated; failed invocations are not automatically retried. Runs may overlap. Design catch-up processing from durable pending work, with claims/leases, idempotency and a last-success marker. A morning cover notice should follow the committed cover event promptly, with scheduled reconciliation as a safety net. [Managing cron jobs](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs)

### Pro baseline

- **$20 USD/month platform fee**, including one deploying team seat and **$20/month usage credit**
- Additional deploying Owner/Member seats: **$20/month each**; Viewer seats are free
- Usage beyond product inclusions and the applicable credit, plus selected add-ons, increases the invoice
- Listed USD prices exclude VAT/GST/other applicable tax; invoice tax depends on billing address

Teachers using the school application are not automatically Vercel deploying seats. Budget for people who actually need provider deployment/admin access. Do not add the included usage credit to the base fee a second time. [Pro plan, updated 15 September 2026](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan)

## 2. Supabase: Free/Pro and separate recovery of records and objects

### Relevant published allowances

| Item | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Plan subscription | $0 | From $25 USD/month |
| Database allocation | 500 MB database size per project | 8 GB provisioned general-purpose disk per project; extra disk $0.125/GB-month |
| Monthly active users | 50,000 | 100,000; then $0.00325/MAU |
| Uncached egress | 5 GB | 250 GB; then $0.09/GB |
| Cached egress | 5 GB | 250 GB; then $0.03/GB |
| Object storage | 1 GB | 100 GB; then $0.0213/GB-month |
| Automatic DB backups | Not included | Daily, seven-day retention |
| Inactivity pausing | After one week | No inactivity pausing |

Free permits two active free projects subject to the account/member quota. Cached and uncached egress are distinct allowances; cached egress applies to Storage. The plan is organization-level; egress and object storage usage aggregate across projects. Free database size and paid provisioned disk size are different billing concepts. [Pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing), [billing scope and pausing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-faq), [disk billing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/disk-size), [object-storage billing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/storage-size)

Paid organizations receive **$10/month compute credit**, approximately covering one Micro instance. Micro is **$0.01344/hour** (about $10/month); additional active projects add their own hourly compute charge. Credit is per organization, resets monthly and does not accumulate. Compute is charged for active runtime, even with little database activity, and **is not covered by the Spend Cap**. [Compute usage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/compute)

Pro's Spend Cap is enabled by default for covered usage; it is not an all-in invoice ceiling or a promise of uninterrupted service. Continued quota exceedance can cause restrictions. Free and Pro do not include an uptime SLA in the published plan comparison. Supabase invoices are USD; conversion and applicable billing-address tax require an invoice/payment-method check. [Billing FAQ](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-faq), [plan comparison](https://supabase.com/pricing)

### Recovery and archives

Database backups retain Storage metadata but **do not back up stored object bytes**. Restoring the DB cannot recover a subsequently deleted PDF/photo. Free projects need planned exports/off-site DB backups; Pro daily backups still allow up to a day's lost work. Point-in-time recovery is an extra paid option, starting around **$100/month for seven-day retention**, and requires at least Small compute. [Backup scope, retention and PITR](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups)

Plan a separate validated object-copy/restore process and a year-close manifest with file checksums, record counts and reconciliation totals. School-owned private Drive can be an archive destination only after ownership, capacity, access and restore procedure are established. Its current plan/cost was not verified. Do not delete financial ledgers or issued reports simply to stay under a free quota.

## 3. Cloudflare: a scheduler option and a separate full-app migration

### Workers cost/limits

- Free: **100,000 requests/day**, **10 ms CPU per invocation**
- Workers Paid Standard: **$5 USD/month minimum per account**, **10 million requests/month** and **30 million CPU-ms/month** included
- Above those allowances: **$0.30/additional million requests** and **$0.02/additional million CPU-ms**
- Workers has no additional egress/bandwidth charge under this pricing; separate products/add-ons have their own charges

[Workers pricing, updated 2 October 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/)

CPU is active computation, not time waiting for network responses. Free Cron has the same 10 ms CPU limit. Paid HTTP defaults to 30 seconds CPU, configurable up to five minutes; Paid Cron is limited to **30 seconds CPU when scheduled more frequently than hourly**, or **15 minutes CPU for hourly-or-slower schedules**. Memory is 128 MB per isolate; cron trigger limits are **5 Free / 250 Paid per account**. Use the interval-specific limits rather than interpreting the pricing page's headline “15 minutes” as applying to every Cron job. [Workers limits, updated 5 September 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/)

Cron supports minute-level schedules, uses UTC, and configuration changes can take up to 15 minutes to propagate. Store school rules in Asia/Kolkata and derive the intended execution times deliberately. [Cron triggers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/cron-triggers/)

### Tradeoff

A small Worker that wakes a narrowly authenticated existing-app reconciliation endpoint is a reasonable option to benchmark. Its own CPU may be light, but the called Vercel/Supabase work still consumes those providers' resources. Adding it does not establish Hobby eligibility. It also adds another credential boundary, deployment, log surface and failure path; provider setup/access changes are outside this planning task.

A full app move needs a separate compatibility proof for Server Actions/auth cookies, fee commands, Supabase integration, PDF/font rendering, images, caches, public capability links and PWA behavior. Cloudflare's current Next.js guide recommends **vinext**, expressly labels it **beta**, and advises compatibility checks for existing production apps. Do not assume a zero-effort or automatically cheaper migration, or rely on an older blanket OpenNext recommendation without rechecking the selected path. [Current Next.js guide, updated 25 August 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/nextjs/)

## 5. Illustrative budgeting worksheet, not the school's bill

4. **AiSensy INR/month, conditional October scenario** = chosen plan + extra seats/add-ons + 1.09 × M + 0.145 × (U + A) + 0.145 × sum over WhatsApp numbers of max(S − 1,000, 0). M/U/A/S are eligible chargeable delivered India messages after applicable exemptions; classify/count from actual provider records. This is not a recommendation to upgrade or a forecast of the school's invoice. [Vercel baseline](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/pro-plan), [Supabase project billing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-faq), [Workers baseline](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/)
